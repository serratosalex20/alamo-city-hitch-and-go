Card Security — Read Before Writing Anything Down Never write, photograph, or store the full card number, CVV, or PIN — on paper or digitally. Card-network rules (PCI) prohibit it and a leaked full number is a breach you are liable for. Record the last 4 digits only. To keep a card on file, save it inside Stripe (customer profile) and charge through Stripe — never from a written copy.
