7. Renter's Responsibilities

  1. Tow Vehicle & Hitch. Renter certifies the tow vehicle and hitch are rated, equipped, and maintained to safely tow the Equipment at its loaded weight, including a properly rated ball/coupler, crossed safety chains, and functioning trailer lights before departure.
  2. Safe Operation. Renter shall operate the Equipment lawfully and safely, secure all cargo, observe the Equipment's rated load capacity (GVWR), and comply with the Texas Transportation Code and all applicable laws.
  3. Care of Equipment. Renter is responsible for the Equipment from release until return and inspection, including theft, vandalism, collision, road hazard, and weather damage. Renter shall keep the Equipment locked/secured when unattended.
  4. Breakdown or Defect. If the Equipment develops a defect or is involved in any accident, Renter shall stop use immediately and notify Owner. Continued use after a known defect is at Renter's sole risk and expense. Renter shall not arrange repairs without Owner's prior written consent.
  5. Return Clean. Renter shall return the Equipment swept clean and free of trash, mud, debris, and material residue, with all accessories listed in Section 2.

8. Prohibited Uses

Renter shall not, and shall not permit anyone to:

  1. operate the tow vehicle or Equipment under the influence of alcohol or any controlled substance;
  2. exceed the Equipment's rated load capacity (GVWR) or load it in an unbalanced manner;
  3. transport hazardous materials, illegal cargo, or persons in or on the Equipment;
  4. take the Equipment outside the State of Texas without Owner's prior written consent;
  5. sublease the Equipment or permit its use by any person not named in this Agreement;
  6. modify, alter, or repair the Equipment; or
  7. use the Equipment in any manner that would void or impair any applicable insurance.

9. Extensions & Late Return

  1. Extensions. Extensions must be requested and approved before the scheduled return time and are sold in the same block sizes as the original rental (Half Day, Full Day, 1 Week, 2 Weeks) at standard published rates, subject to availability.
  2. Late Fee. If the Equipment is returned after the scheduled return time without an approved extension, Renter shall pay a flat late fee of $100.00 plus the applicable block rate for the additional time held.
  3. Failure to Return. Equipment not returned within 24 hours of the scheduled return time, where Renter cannot be reached, may be reported to law enforcement as stolen, and Renter shall be liable for the full replacement value of the Equipment plus recovery costs.

10. Cleaning, Damage & Loss

  1. Cleaning Fee. Equipment returned in a soiled condition requiring more than approximately fifteen (15) minutes of cleanup incurs a flat cleaning fee of $100.00, documented photographically.
  2. Damage / Loss. Renter shall pay the reasonable cost to repair any damage occurring during the rental term (normal wear excepted), or the fair replacement value of the Equipment or any accessory that is lost, stolen, or damaged beyond economical repair, less any insurance proceeds actually received by Owner. Renter shall promptly file a police report for any theft or vandalism.
  3. Inspection. The parties will jointly inspect the Equipment at pickup and at return and record its condition on the Condition Report (final page). Renter's signature on the Condition Report confirms the recorded condition.

11. Liability, Indemnity & General Terms

  1. As-Is. The Equipment is provided “AS IS.” Owner disclaims all warranties, express or implied, including merchantability and fitness for a particular purpose, to the maximum extent permitted by law.
  2. Indemnification. Renter shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless Owner and its members, employees, and agents from all claims, damages, liabilities, costs, and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising from Renter's possession or use of the Equipment or breach of this Agreement, except to the extent caused by Owner's gross negligence or willful misconduct.
  3. Limitation of Liability. Owner's total liability shall not exceed the amounts paid by Renter under this rental. Owner is not liable for indirect, incidental, or consequential damages, including lost profits, lost time, or towing costs, nor for personal property left in or on the Equipment.
  4. Governing Law & Venue. This Agreement is governed by Texas law. Exclusive venue for any dispute is the state courts of Bexar County, Texas.
  5. Entire Agreement; Severability. This Agreement (with its Condition Report) is the entire agreement between the parties. Amendments must be in writing and signed by both parties. If any provision is unenforceable, the remainder continues in effect.
